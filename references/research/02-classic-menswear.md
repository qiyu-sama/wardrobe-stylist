## A. 西装外套的成文规则

### A1. 驳头宽度与身形的比例关系

- 驳头宽度在历史上大幅波动，不是固定值。1930s 与 1970s 极宽；1950s 末至 1960s 大部分极窄，**"often only about 1 inch (2.5 cm) wide"**。2000s 中期至 2010s 趋势为更窄的驳头 + 更高的驳头缺口（gorge）+ 相应更高的扣位 + 更短的衣长；2020s 驳头渐宽、衣长回归传统，但 gorge 与扣位仍高。
  引文："The width of the lapel is a varying aspect of suits and has changed over the years. The 1930s and 1970s featured exceptionally wide lapels, whereas during the late 1950s and most of the 1960s suits with very narrow lapels—often only about 1 inch (2.5 cm) wide—were in fashion."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 领带宽度**通常跟随**驳头宽度。"Necktie width usually follows the width of the jacket lapel."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 唯一一条把驳头宽度与身体比例挂钩的成文表述，来自设计师观点（非硬规则）：驳头"应当延伸到衣领线与肩线之间中点稍内的位置"。
  引文："Some designers maintain however that most stylish lapel width does not change, and that the lapel 'should extend to just a fraction less than the halfway mark between the collar and shoulder line'."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacket_lapel
  → 这是按**肩宽**定的比例锚点，不是按胖瘦。抓到的材料里**没有**"胖人用宽驳头/瘦人用窄驳头"这类成文规则（见「未取得」）。
- 驳头形状与门襟的对应：平驳（notched）通常只出现在单排扣上，是三种里最不正式的；戗驳（peaked）通常配双排扣，单排扣也有但罕见；青果领（shawl）源自维多利亚时期非正式晚装，除 tuxedo/dinner suit 外一般不出现在西装上。
  引文："Notched lapels, the most common of the three, are usually only found on single-breasted jackets and are the most informal style. They are distinguished by a 75- to 90-degree 'notch' at the point where the lapel meets the collar."
  引文："Double-breasted jackets usually have peaked lapels, although peaked lapels are sometimes found on single-breasted jackets as well."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 单排扣戗驳头是最难的裁缝活之一。"The ability to properly cut peaked lapels on a single-breasted suit is one of the most challenging tailoring tasks, even for very experienced tailors."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)

### A2. 单排扣 vs 双排扣的扣位规则

- 扣位（button stance）= 纽扣高度相对自然腰线的位置，决定整体扣法，先于任何通则。
  引文："The buttoning of the jacket is primarily determined by the button stance, a measure of how high the buttons are in relation to the natural waist."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 数量通例：单排扣多为 2 或 3 颗，4 颗及以上罕见（zoot suit 可 6 颗以上）；dinner jacket 通常只 1 颗。**"The centre or top button will typically line up quite closely with the natural waistline."**
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 单排扣 2 颗：最下一颗传统上不扣，衣服本身就是按"扣了就破坏线条与垂坠"来裁的。站立时扣、坐下时解开。
  引文："The bottom button is usually not meant to be buttoned and so the jacket is cut such that buttoning the bottom button would ruin the lines and drape of the jacket. It is customary to keep the jacket buttoned while standing and to unbutton the jacket while seated."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 单排扣 3 颗：扣中间那颗，最上一颗有时扣，最下一颗传统上**根本没设计成可扣**。
  引文："When fastening a three-button suit, the middle button is fastened, and the top one sometimes, but the bottom is traditionally not designed to be."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 双排扣：外侧纽扣只有一半是功能扣，第二排纯装饰，所以必然成对出现。**6 颗为典型，2 颗可扣**；最后一对浮在搭门之上。4 颗双排扣通常扣成方形。
  引文："Double-breasted jackets have only half their outer buttons functional, as the second row is for display only, forcing them to come in pairs. … Six buttons are typical, with two to button; the last pair floats above the overlap."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 双排扣几乎总是保持扣上；若有多个功能扣（传统 six-on-two），**只需扣一颗**，也可只扣下扣以示更长的竖向线条（Duke of Kent 推广）。单排扣坐下时通常解开，双排扣常保持扣着以免反复解内扣（"anchor button"）。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 纽扣布局与驳头形状是**协同**决定视线走向的：扣位过低或驳头卷边过重，视线会被拉离面部，并让腰显得更粗。
  引文："if the buttons are too low, or the lapel roll too pronounced, the eyes are drawn down from the face, and the waist appears larger."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 双排扣搭门哪一侧在上："There seems to be no clear rule" —— 通常左侧压外，但并非铁律。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)

### A3. 袖长（衬衫袖口露出多少）

- 成文材料里**没有**给出"露出 x 英寸"的统一硬标准；抓到的最接近的成文表述是一条引用 Savile Row 衬衫裁缝的媒体问答：
  引文："I spoke to Stephen Lachter, the doyen of Savile Row's shirt makers … who said: 'The rule used to be that you would show on the cuff what you would show in the back of the collar.' … Roughly speaking, Lachter feels you should show around half an inch, although apparently David Furnish, for one, likes to have them much longer … Sean O'Flynn, another favourite shirt maker, says that when your jacket is off, the cuff should hit the wrist just above the hand."
  `[成文]` https://www.gq-magazine.co.uk/article/gq-style-shrink-proper-mens-shirt-sleeve-length
  → 可用的量化锚点：**约半英寸（≈1.3cm）**，且"与后领露出的宽度相同"这条旧规则；另有一条以解剖位置定界（不戴外套时袖口到腕骨上缘）。
- 袖口类型与正式度（决定"露出来的是什么"）：barrel cuff（纽扣式，标准）< double/French cuff（折回、用袖扣，较正式）< single cuff（不折回、用袖扣，**最正式**，通常只配 white tie）。
  引文："Single cuffs, the most formal style, usually only worn with formal evening wear (i.e. 'white tie'), are fastened with cuff links but are not folded back."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_shirt
- 袖口的假开扣（surgeon's cuff / working button holes）一旦开孔，袖长基本不能再改。"Certainty in fitting sleeve length must be achieved, as once working button holes are cut, the sleeve length essentially cannot be altered further."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)

### A4. 肩线、胸围松量、衣长

- **肩线与胸围松量的量化标准：未取得。** 抓到的公开成文材料（维基、GQ、V&A）均无量化的肩宽余量/胸围松量数值，这部分通常写在裁缝行内部 measurement guide 里，本次未拿到（Debrett's 与 Savile Row 各家说明页均未取得，见文末）。
- 能拿到的成文表述是**没有数**的定性标准：
  引文："Tailored balance created from a canvas fitting allows a balanced silhouette so a jacket need not be buttoned and a garment is not too tight or too loose. A proper garment is shaped from the neck to the chest and shoulders to drape without wrinkles from tension."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
  → 可归纳为两条可操作判据：①不扣扣子也不变形（"balanced"）；②颈—胸—肩之间没有因张力产生的斜向褶皱。`[推断]` 归纳自上一条。
- 衣长与扣位、gorge 是联动的：mid-2000s–2010s 流行"更高的 gorge + 更高的扣位 + 更短的衣长"；2020s 衣长回归传统但扣位仍高（见 A1）。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 结构件：服装的"形"来自 canvas 衬（而非粘合），"Shape is the essential part of tailoring that often takes hand work from the start."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 开衩：单衩 / 无衩 / 双衩三种。无衩被认为属于意大利做法，双衩典型属英式；dinner jacket 传统上无衩；钩形衩（hook vent）是美国 Ivy 特征。
  引文："Ventless jackets are associated with Italian tailoring, while the double-vented style is typically British. … Dinner jackets traditionally have no vents."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)

### A5. 上装与下装的正式度对应：腰带 / 吊带（braces）

- 黑领结（black tie）：**裤腰永远不配皮带**；用被马甲遮住的吊带支撑；裤子无翻边（uncuffed）、无裤袢（belt loops），外侧缝一道丝缎辫。
  引文："Black tie trousers traditionally have no turn-ups, also called cuffs in American English, or belt loops. … Belts are never worn with black tie trousers."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tie
- 日间礼服（morning dress）：裤子有翻边是**错的**；腰带"should not be worn with morning dress"；吊带可穿，用来防止腰头从马甲下露出来。
  引文："Formal trousers should not have turn-ups … Belts should not be worn with morning dress."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morning_dress
- 白领结（white tie）：马甲不得从燕尾服前摆下方露出来，因此需要中腰或高腰，并"often suspenders (braces) for the trousers"。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_tie
- 马甲扣法：最下一颗传统上不扣（与外套开衩同理，为了坐下时身体能弯）。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 背带 vs 皮带的功能论证（成文但属论证而非禁令）：背带从肩部受力，裤子总能以正确位置悬挂；皮带会让裤腰滑到胯上或凸肚下方，且需要不断调整；背带还能配略松的腰围，给坐姿留出空间。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
  - 历史补充：皮带原本不与西装同穿，战时弹性材料短缺促成了皮带普及。
    `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suit_(clothing)
- 日常西装（lounge suit）本身：**没有**"必须或不许配腰带"的成文禁令 —— 只有礼服类（black tie / white tie / morning dress）有明确禁令。`[推断]` 归纳自 A5 前四条（禁令只出现在礼服三节，lounge suit 节无对应条文）。

## B. 上装—下装—鞋的正式度阶梯

### B1. 阶梯本身

维基 Dress code 词条的「Western dress codes」导航栏给出一条自上而下的完整阶梯（这是条目自带的分类），逐档对应衣物如下 `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_code ：

| 档 | 英文档名 | 男装对应物（条目所列） |
|---|---|---|
| 最正式 | Formal（full dress） | White tie / Morning dress / Full dress uniform / Frock coat / Evening gown / Ball gown |
| 次正式 | Semi-formal（half dress） | Black tie / Black lounge suit / Mess dress uniform / Evening gown / Cocktail dress |
| 非正式 | Informal（"dress clothes"） | Suit / Service dress uniform / Cocktail dress |
| 半非正式 | Semi-informal（Uniform, undress, "professional casual"） | Combat uniform / Smart casual / Business casual / Workwear / School uniform（academic dress） |
| 休闲 | Casual | Casual Friday / Street style / Sportswear / Swimwear / Nightwear |

- 词条正文另有两条定性表述：
  引文（formal）："The most-formal dress code is a full-length ball or evening gowns with evening gloves for women and for men white tie, which also includes a tailcoat."
  引文（semi-formal）："'Semi-formal' has a much less precise definition but typically means an evening jacket and tie for men (known as black tie) and a dress for women."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_code
- 同一档内也有细分级：frilled/patterned 白衬衫比素白衬衫正式，黑色领结比素色 cravat 正式，但三者"都可接受"。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_code
- 日间礼仪分档：morning dress 之下是 **black lounge suit / stroller / Stresemann**（灰条纹 morning trousers + 及常规长度的 lounge 外套，黑/午夜蓝/灰，单排或双排）。至今仍在法律行业（barrister）与共济会日间仪式中出现；条纹礼裤在某些圈子里被直接叫作 "barrister trousers"。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_lounge_suit

### B2. 时间规则（这条是硬规则，且被多方重复）

- **18:00 分界**：Debrett's 明确说 morning dress 不应被指定为 18:00 之后开始的活动着装，此时应指定 white tie。
  引文："Debrett's states that morning dress should not be specified as the dress code for events starting after 18:00 (6:00 p.m.). If a formal event will commence at or after 18:00, white tie should be specified instead."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morning_dress
- black tie 同样有日间禁忌："Black tie is traditionally worn only after six o'clock in the evening, or after sundown during winter months. Black tie's rough daytime equivalent is the [morning dress/black lounge suit]."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tie
- 婚礼时间点：晚间 6 点前的婚礼通常不是 black tie（"Weddings before 6:00 P.M. are not usually black tie unless specified."）。
  `[成文]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tie （词条引用的婚礼指南）

### B3. Business casual / smart casual 的边界

- **"There is no generally agreed definition of 'business casual'."** 该词条把这一点写得非常直白：它兴起于 1990 年代西方白领职场，硅谷 IT 公司是早期采用者；与"西装+领带"（international standard business attire）相对；解释因组织而异，是职场着装困惑的常见来源。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_casual
- 具体化定义（多方，彼此不完全一致）：
  - 含卡其裤、slacks、裙、短袖 polo 与长袖衬衫；排除紧身/超短裙、T 恤、卫衣。
  - 中性色偏深浅黑灰蓝，可含白与米白；"the clothing should be pressed and have clean, crisp seams"（熨过、缝线利落）。
  - 多伦多大学 "Dress for Success" 定义："a classic, clean cut, and put together look where a full suit is not required"，即 slacks/卡其/裙 + 有领衬衫（**不系领带**）+ 针织 + 包头鞋；并警告 "Don't let the word casual mislead you. You still need to look professional."
  - 2011 年一篇报道建议的 "safe global standard"："a button down shirt," "jackets or blazers, khaki or gray slacks, and leather shoes."
  - blue jeans 是否可接受因公司而异，部分企业视其为 sloppy。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_casual
- smart casual 与 business casual 的界限：自 1950s 起"virtually undistinguishable"，smart casual 默认指"比传统深色厚呢西装更随意的西装"；差异主要在领带、气候暖时甚至在有外套与否。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_casual
- 邮轮与场所的实际硬约束例子：某些场合 blazers、衬衫、领带可接受，但**短裤、牛仔裤、T 恤对男性不可接受**。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_casual
- 著名一句（关于宁过勿欠）："it is far more embarrassing to be under-dressed than over"。
  `[成文]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smart_casual

### B4. 各国/各行业差异（抓到的部分）

- 美国：正式度以 black tie 为常见上限，white tie 几乎绝迹（军队条例里 white tie 场合在美国"almost none … outside the national capital region"）。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_wear
- 军队内部规范化例子：美军军官 black tie 需配 turndown collar + 黑领带 + 黑 cummerbund；white tie 需 wing-collar + 白领带 + 白马甲；两种场合唯一指定外衣都是 army blue cape。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_wear
- 英国：black lounge suit 现属罕见，但仍在律师业（barrister）与共济会日间仪式保留。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_lounge_suit
- 瑞典/芬兰：白领结用于学术场合时，白天改配黑色马甲。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_tie
- 苏格兰裙等民族服饰可替代 white tie："explicitly state that national costume or national dress may be substituted for white tie."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_wear

## C. 历史与物证

### C1. 燕尾服（dress coat / tailcoat）的结构

- 两大分支（21 世纪存留）：**dress coat**（晚装，前摆方角直切 squarely cut away，配 white tie）与 **morning coat**（日装，前摆渐次斜切 gradually tapered，配 morning dress）。俗称不加限定时，tailcoat 指前者。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tailcoat
- 结构细节（关键的可复用判据）：
  引文："A dress coat is waist length in the front and sides, and has two long tails reaching to the knees in back. … The front of the skirt is squarely cut away. Since around the 1830s the coat has been constructed with a waist seam that allows greater waist suppression. From the Victorian era, the revers has taken facings in silk (grosgrain or satin) on the lapels. Although it is double-breasted, since the 1870s, the dress coat no longer fastens in the front. … although there are two rows of buttons, these are all non-functional, serving only a decorative function."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tailcoat
  → **双排扣 + 不扣合 + 纽扣全为装饰**，这是 dress coat 最反直觉的一条，与 lounge suit 的扣法规则完全无关。
- 同源的日/夜差异，用腰缝切线区分：晨礼服腰线**斜切（obliquely）**，晚礼服腰线**水平切（horizontally）**，燕尾的裁法也不同（swallow tail）。晨礼服的裙部结构源自骑马。
  引文："The cutaway front of the morning tail coat differs from the evening tail coat (dress coat) in that the waist of the former is cut obliquely while the waist of the latter is cut horizontally, and the tail is cut differently from the swallow tailcoat used for evening dress. The skirt waist construction of the coats is equestrian in origin, to ease the wearer's riding his horse."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morning_dress
- 晨礼服外套（morning coat）：现代做法**总是单排扣**，1 颗扣（极少 2 颗）或链式扣，戗驳头；腰缝上两枚装饰纽扣；驳头通常为戗驳而非平驳（因为现在只作礼服穿；刚出现时它还只是 half dress，所以平驳常见）。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tailcoat

### C2. 白领结（white tie）的完整结构 —— 以 Debrett's 为据

逐件（引用的是一手礼仪权威的内容，经词条转述）：
1. 白 marcella 衬衫，翼领，**单袖口（single cuffs）**，以 studs 与 cufflinks 固定
2. 白 marcella 领结
3. 低领口 marcella 马甲
4. 黑色 **双排扣** barathea 羊毛或超细 herringbone 燕尾服，丝面戗驳
5. 裤：两腿外侧各**两道 galon（辫条）**
6. 鞋：**patent leather court shoes**
7. 白手套、高礼帽、手杖、斗篷现已罕见

引文："According to the British etiquette guide Debrett's, the central components of full evening dress for men are a white marcella shirt with a wing collar and single cuffs, fastened with studs and cufflinks; the eponymous white marcella bow tie is worn around the collar, while a low-cut marcella waistcoat is worn over the shirt. Over this is worn a black double-breasted barathea wool or ultrafine herringbone tailcoat with silk faced peak lapels. The trousers have two galon down the outside of both legs. The correct shoes are patent leather court shoes."
`[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_tie

配套的比例/视觉规则：
- 马甲不得外露；GQ 的总结是 "The simple rule of thumb is that you should only ever see black and white not black, white and black again."（黑—白—黑的三段视觉，不能出现第二次黑）
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_tie
- 袖口分歧：Debrett's 接受双袖口，但多数裁缝与商家认为单袖口（用袖扣）才是最传统、最正式的；"Double cuffs are not frequently worn or recommended with white tie."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_tie
- 19 世纪中后期，"黑白色系"被编码为上层阶级晚 6 点后正式场合的标准配色。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_tie

### C3. 黑领结（black tie）的完整结构

传统配件清单（词条原文）`[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tie ：
- dinner jacket：黑或午夜蓝羊毛，丝面（grosgrain 或 satin）驳头；最传统面料是羊毛 barathea 或超细 herringbone
- 裤：外侧缝单道丝缎辫、无翻边、无裤袢、用吊带、配黑皮带禁令
- 腰：低领口马甲**或** cummerbund，**二者绝不同时穿**
- 衬衫：白，marcella 或 pleated 前胸，双袖口（French cuffs），turndown 领
- 黑丝领结，与驳头丝面相配
- studs + cufflinks（经典礼仪权威限制 studs 只用于硬挺 marcella 前胸，软前胸用珍珠扣）
- 黑丝袜（有指南建议及膝）
- 黑鞋：最传统为 patent opera pumps（带 grosgrain 蝴蝶结）；现多用无雕花的牛津鞋

成文禁令（这是 D 节的主要素材来源）：
- "Brogueing or any other decorative patterns should never be seen on black tie footwear."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tie
- 马甲与 cummerbund 二选一，不能同时。
- 马甲**绝不与双排扣外套同穿**（双排扣从不解扣，裤腰永不外露，无需遮盖）；cummerbund 同理。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tie
- 青果领被认为较不正式、平驳最不正式；但三者历史上都出现过。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tie
- 英式 20 世纪礼仪：白色 dinner jacket **在英国境内任何天气都不穿**，只留给出国时穿。"white dinner jackets were never worn, even on the hottest day of summer, but were reserved for wear abroad."
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tie
- 机构差异：Debrett's 认为腰饰（马甲/cummerbund）已非必需；美国 Emily Post Institute 则视其为正装必需件。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tie
- open lacing 的 Derby/blucher 被认为对 black tie 太随意。
  `[物证]` https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_tie
